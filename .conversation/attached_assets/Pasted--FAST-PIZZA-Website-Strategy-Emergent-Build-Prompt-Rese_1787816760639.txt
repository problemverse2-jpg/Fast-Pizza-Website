## 1. Business Analysis
- **Offer:** quick-service pizza — dine-in, drive-through, delivery, open until 3 AM [Source: GBP listing].
- **Audience:** Poornima University/Institute students next door — a ~32-acre campus [Source: web search] — plus Mahatma Gandhi Hospital staff and visitors across the road [Source: GBP listing], and Sitapura residents / late-shift workers.
- **Sourced strengths:** 4.2★ across 25 reviews [Source: GBP listing]; repeated praise for delivery speed and food quality [Source: GBP reviews]; garlic knots called out by name as a standout [Source: GBP reviews].
- **Friction to solve:** one visible review flags arrogant/uninformed staff [Source: GBP reviews] — copy should lean on speed and food quality, the consistently repeated theme, and avoid claiming "hospitality" or "best service," since that has a documented counter-example.
- **Digital gap = opportunity:** zero footprint beyond GBP itself [Source: web search]. Every order today walks in, calls, or gets lucky on Maps. A site plus a direct WhatsApp ordering path captures demand that currently leaks to whoever answers the phone first.
- **Primary conversion goal:** Order on WhatsApp / Call Now (Restaurant category × Tier A).

## 2. Website Concept
Fast, bold, appetite-driven quick-service energy — closer to a Domino's-style ordering flow than a sit-down restaurant site. **Light theme only, no dark mode** — this business doesn't need the extra design and QA surface, and skipping it ships faster.

## 3. Color Palette (light only)
| Role | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#D62828` | appetite red, QSR convention |
| Secondary | `#1F1B18` | charcoal — carries all body text |
| Accent | `#F4A300` | cheese-gold — CTAs, hover |
| Background | `#FFFBF5` | Surface | `#FFFFFF` |
| Muted text | `#6B6560` | Button hover | `#B21F1F` |

Red-on-cream fails AA at body-text sizes, so red is restricted to large text/accents; charcoal-on-cream carries all body copy at >7:1 contrast.

## 4. Typography
- Heading: **Baloo 2** (rounded, high-weight, standard in Indian QSR branding)
- Body: **Inter** (legible at small sizes for the menu/price grid)
- Button: Inter, semibold, uppercase, slight letter-tracking

## 6. Structure
**Home · Menu · Contact** — three pages, deliberately lean. A QSR site converts on speed-to-order, not page depth; more pages here is scope creep, not value.
- **6A:** standard breakpoints (320/640/768/1024/1280).
- **6B:** Tier A — no backend data layer beyond the contact/order relay.
- **6C:** no admin area.

## 7–8. Homepage & Copy
Hero leads with the two claims the reviews actually support: fast, and open till 3 AM. Primary CTA "Order on WhatsApp," secondary "Call Now." Menu preview pulls from the same category data as the full Menu page. Prices are `[PLACEHOLDER — confirm with client]` — GBP doesn't expose an actual price list. Reviews section quotes only the two attributable positive reviews already public [Source: GBP reviews] — no invented ratings, awards, or quotes.

## 9–11. Imagery, Motion, Mobile
Warm close-up food photography (cheese pulls, shared pizza) — stock/placeholder until real photos are supplied. Standard reveal/hover motion, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. **Sticky "Order on WhatsApp" button on mobile** — the single highest-leverage element on the site, since the real customer is ordering from a phone at 11 PM, not browsing a desktop menu.

## 12–13. SEO, Accessibility, Security
`Restaurant`/`LocalBusiness` schema. NAP must match the GBP listing character-for-character, including the "near Mahatma Gandhi Hospital, behind Poornima Institute" landmark phrasing — mismatched NAP is the most common reason a listing like this underperforms once a site exists. Full WCAG 2.2 AA, full security header set, Redis-backed rate limiting on the contact/order relay, honeypot field. No admin surface to harden, since none exists.

## 13C. Monitoring
Sentry on frontend + relay function. Every WhatsApp/call-intent click and form submission also logged to a fallback Sheet — if a WhatsApp deep link fails to open on some device, the lead still isn't lost.

## 15. AI Integration
Included: a small chat bubble answering "are you open now," "what's on the menu," "is there a veg option," "where exactly are you" — grounded strictly in `site.ts`, deferring anything else to WhatsApp. Genuinely useful at 1–2 AM when no one's picking up the phone.

## 16. Final Emergent Prompt

Build a production-ready marketing website for FAST PIZZA (फास्ट पिज्जा), a quick-service pizza restaurant in Sitapura, Jaipur, at Shop No. S1, 92, MGH Road, near Mahatma Gandhi Hospital, behind Poornima Institute. The business has no existing website — every customer today finds it through Google Maps, walk-in, or word of mouth — so treat this as the business's first digital storefront: fast to load, obvious to order from, and honest about what it's actually known for.

The location sits opposite Mahatma Gandhi Hospital and beside the ~32-acre Poornima University/Institute campus, and is open until 3 AM — a genuinely rare trait in this category that almost no nearby competitor can claim. Lead with it. The available public data — a 4.2-star rating across 25 reviews, repeated praise for delivery speed and food quality, a standout mention of the garlic knots, and one complaint about staff attitude — should shape every line of copy: sell speed and food quality, the consistent, repeated theme; never sell "hospitality" or "best service," the one theme with a documented counter-example. Do not invent ratings, awards, menu prices, or staff details. Anywhere real data is missing — menu prices, food photography, logo — insert a clearly commented placeholder for the client to fill in before launch; never fabricate it.

Build three pages only: Home, Menu, Contact. This is deliberate — a quick-service restaurant converts on speed-to-order, not page depth. Home carries a hero (headline built on "fast" plus late-night hours), a menu preview drawing from the same category data as the full Menu page, a "why fast pizza" section built only from the sourced praise themes, an hours/location block with an embedded map, the two attributable review quotes, and a final CTA. Every page carries a persistent, sticky "Order on WhatsApp" action on mobile — the single highest-value UI element on the site, since the real customer is a student or hospital visitor ordering from a phone late at night, not browsing a desktop menu.

Use a red/charcoal/cheese-gold palette on one light theme only (no dark mode — unnecessary scope here), Baloo 2 for headings, Inter for body copy and the menu/price grid. Respect the WCAG AA contrast pairings specified: red for large text and accents only, charcoal-on-cream for all body copy.

This is Tier A: every "order" and "contact" action is a lead-capture relay to WhatsApp/email, not a live transactional system. Copy must say "Order on WhatsApp" or "Call to Order," never imply a real-time cart, checkout, or delivery tracking that doesn't exist. There is no admin area and no login. Implement the one AI feature specified below: a small FAQ/concierge chat widget answering hours, menu, veg/non-veg, and location questions, grounded strictly in `site.ts`, deferring everything else to WhatsApp — this business operates hours when nobody's available to answer the phone, and a grounded widget covers that gap cheaply and safely.

Treat local SEO as first-class, not an afterthought: `Restaurant`/`LocalBusiness` schema, and NAP that matches the GBP listing exactly, character for character, including the "near Mahatma Gandhi Hospital, behind Poornima Institute" landmark language — mismatched NAP is the most common reason a listing like this underperforms in local search once a website exists. Follow the full performance, accessibility, security, monitoring, and CI/CD requirements below without exception — none are optional at this business size; they're what separates a real production build from a template.

Build this as a React + Vite + TypeScript project. Centralize ALL
business-specific info into src/config/site.ts, exported as named

component imports from this file — no business info hardcoded
elsewhere.

At the top of site.ts, read optional overrides from URL query
parameters, falling back to defaults:

const params = new URLSearchParams(window.location.search);
const override = (key: string, fallback: string) => params.get(key) || fallback;

Wire up overrides for at minimum: name, shortName, tagline, phone,
phoneE164, city, address, whatsappNumber.
Defaults: name "Fast Pizza", phone "080002 11303", phoneE164
"+918000211303", city "Jaipur", address "Shop No. S 1, 92, MGH Road,
near Mahatma Gandhi Hospital, behind Poornima Institute, Sitapura,
Jaipur, Rajasthan 302022", whatsappNumber same as phoneE164 unless
the client supplies a different WhatsApp-for-business number.

Regardless of query params present, render a canonical tag pointing
to the base URL without query params:
<link rel="canonical" href="https://yoursite.com/" />

Breakpoints: mobile (base) 320-639px, sm 640-767px, md 768-1023px
